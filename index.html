How this was measured

Satellite greenness: NASA MODIS MOD13Q1 and MYD13Q1 NDVI, 250 m, from the ORNL DAAC subset service. Rain: Open-Meteo historical weather (ERA5 reanalysis) at the Dish. The colours are a gold-to-green ramp scaled by NDVI, not photographs; gold is also lighter than green, so the rows read without colour vision.
